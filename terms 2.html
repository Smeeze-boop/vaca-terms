1. Program description

Vaca is a private scheduling tool operated by nebr.xr@gmail.com (the "Organizer") for a small group of people who take turns choosing annual vacation weeks in an ordered "draft." The Program sends text messages that tell a member when it is their turn to choose, include a private link to the schedule, send reminders if a member has not yet chosen, and announce when the draft is complete. The Program does not send marketing or promotional messages.

2. Eligibility and consent

The Program is open only to members of the Organizer's private group. You will receive text messages only after you have agreed in writing (by email reply) to receive them and have provided your mobile number. Agreeing to receive text messages is voluntary and is not a condition of taking part in the draft; members who decline are contacted by email instead. Your consent applies only to this Program.

3. Message frequency and costs

Message frequency varies: about 1 to 5 messages per turn, and only while the draft is running. Message and data rates may apply. The Program is free, but your mobile carrier may charge for text messages and data according to your plan.

4. Opting out and getting help

You may withdraw your consent at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages. For help, reply HELP or contact nebr.xr@gmail.com. To resume messages after opting out, contact nebr.xr@gmail.com.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Use the Program only for its intended purpose and follow the draft rules set by the Organizer.
  • Check the schedule page yourself if you do not receive a message; the schedule page is the official record, not the texts.
