Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the draft: your name, your mobile phone number, your consent to receive texts, the vacation weeks you choose, and a record of the messages sent to you with their delivery status.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to run the draft: to send you the messages described in the Terms and Conditions, to record and display the schedule to group members, and to respond to your requests.

3. Sharing

Mobile information and opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers that deliver the Program, namely our text messaging provider (Twilio) and our hosting provider, solely so they can deliver messages and run the website, or when required by law. Group members can see names or initials on the shared schedule, but not phone numbers.

4. Retention

We keep your information for as long as the group uses the Program, and remove your phone number and related message records on request.

5. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including private personal links and a password-protected administrator page. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may stop text messages at any time by replying STOP, and you may ask to see, correct or delete your information by contacting nebr.xr@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: nebr.xr@gmail.com.
